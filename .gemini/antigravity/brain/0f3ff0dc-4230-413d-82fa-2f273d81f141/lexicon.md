# TelPy Lexicon Draft

## Philosophy
- **Romanization**: Modified ISO 15919 for readability (e.g., 'ee' for long i, 'oo' for long u, 'th' for dental t).
- **Semantics**: Preference for action verbs and common colloquial terms over formal Sanskrit.
